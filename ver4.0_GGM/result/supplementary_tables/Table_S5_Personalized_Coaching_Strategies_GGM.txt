KEY INSIGHTS FOR PERSONALIZED COACHING:
======================================================================================================================================================

1. MetS-SPECIFIC PATTERNS:
   • Fried Foods: Universal in MetS(+) groups [5/5 = 100%] vs 2/3 = 66.7% in MetS(-)
     → PRIMARY intervention target for MetS patients

2. HEALTH MAINTENANCE PATTERNS:
   • Protein Foods: Universal in MetS(-) groups [6/6 = 100%] vs 2/5 = 40% in MetS(+)
     → KEY for maintaining metabolic health

3. UNIQUE GROUP-SPECIFIC HUBS:
   • Sweet Foods: Appear ONLY in middle-aged MetS(+) women → gender-specific intervention
   • Sugar-Sweetened Beverages: Appear ONLY in elderly MetS(+) men → critical diabetes risk
   • Vegetables: Appear in youth MetS(+) men and elderly MetS(-) women → positive patterns to reinforce

4. EXEMPLARY PATTERN:
   • Elderly MetS(-) women: Protein + Vegetables, sparsest network (density=0.045)
     → This is the TARGET pattern for all other groups to aspire to

5. INTERVENTION PRIORITY TIERS:
   • VERY HIGH: Elderly M_MetS+ (SSB risk), Middle F_MetS+ (sweet foods risk)
   • HIGH: Youth M_MetS+ (fried foods), Middle M_MetS+ (fried foods), Elderly F_MetS+ (high-fat meat)
   • MODERATE: Youth M_MetS-, Youth F_MetS-, Middle F_MetS-
   • LOW: Middle M_MetS-, Elderly M_MetS-
   • VERY LOW: Elderly F_MetS- (maintenance only - model pattern)
